What do I do?

I study statistics and data science, but I came into it through international relations and a curiosity about politics. Most of the things I work on sit somewhere between those worlds.

Research and work

I'm currently working on my master's thesis at Statistics Netherlands (CBS), exploring how machine learning can help improve the automatic editing of business survey data.

At Utrecht University, I'm a research student assistant working on speech recognition for recordings that mix languages. I recently wrapped up a traineeship with the Missing Data Group, where I developed an R imputation method combining glmmTMB and mice for multilevel missing data, and tested it through simulations with continuous, overdispersed and zero-inflated outcomes. I've also worked as a data analyst at the Joint Impact Model Foundation / FMO Bank.

Some projects

  • Bayesian Analysis of FIFA Rankings and National Team Performance
    A Bayesian regression project in R using a custom Gibbs sampler and posterior checks.
  • HousingyBot
    A Python tool that monitors rental listings and sends Telegram alerts.
  • Household Income Multiple Imputation
    Using R and Quarto to study income nonresponse across five European countries.
  • Regional Analysis of Child Stunting
    Statistical analysis of household survey data and socioeconomic risk factors.
  • Predicting Cat Weight
    Comparing random forests and linear regression using veterinary data.
  • 5 Minutes to Midnight
    A game-theory simulation about Cold War rivalry and strategic decision-making.

Beyond university

I'm also building ORBIT, an interactive 3D observatory for exploring how countries express their positions on international events. Another work in progress is diploTrace, an R package for measuring diplomatic positions from different sources while accounting for uncertainty.

I enjoy writing Substack articles, experimenting with design, and creating short educational videos on The Polynode.

More code is on my GitHub, and you can find the longer version of my background in my CV.
